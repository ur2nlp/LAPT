# === Bucket A: held-out REAL verses ===========================================
# Drawn from the v1.0.0 translation holdout (leakage-free). Measures genuine
# in-domain translation. The got->eng pairs especially test the conditioning
# failure: does the model reproduce the actual verse, or hallucinate a plausible
# one? The sower verse is the shortest holdout item.

Translate to Gothic: "The farmer sows the word."

Translate to English: 𐍃𐌰 𐍃𐌰𐌹𐌾𐌰𐌽𐌳𐍃 𐍅𐌰𐌿𐍂𐌳 𐍃𐌰𐌹𐌾𐌹𐌸.
Translate to English: 𐌿𐌽𐍄𐌴 𐍃𐌰𐌴𐌹 𐌽𐌹𐍃𐍄 𐍅𐌹𐌸𐍂𐌰 𐌹𐌶𐍅𐌹𐍃, 𐍆𐌰𐌿𐍂 𐌹𐌶𐍅𐌹𐍃 𐌹𐍃𐍄.

# === Bucket B: in-domain CONSTRUCTED ==========================================
# Invented sentences recombining common Biblical vocabulary (lord/frauja,
# disciples/siponjos, Jesus, son/sunus, God/guþ, faith/galaubein, world/fairhvus)
# but NOT verbatim verses. Measures composition on familiar lexis. eng->got only
# for full sentences (authoring novel valid Gothic input reliably is not feasible
# without verified vocab); word lookups probe in-domain lexical coverage.
Translate to Gothic: "The Lord saw his disciples."
Translate to Gothic: Jesus said to the man.
Translate to Gothic: "The son of God came into the world."
What is the Gothic translation for the word "faith"?
What is the Gothic translation for the word "lord"?
Work through this sentence word-by-word, then give its translation into Gothic. English: "The Lord saw his disciples."
